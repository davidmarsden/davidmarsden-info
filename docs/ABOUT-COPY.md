I’m David Marsden. I live in Southall, west London, and this is the place where the various bits of my online life meet: writing, local investigations, community work, music, football worlds, photographs and small web projects that seemed like a good idea at the time.

## What I do

A lot of my current writing is about **local democracy, development, environment and community power in Southall and Ealing**. My main home for that work is [Southall Stories](https://southallstories.uk/), with [Ealing Civic Commons](https://commons.southallstories.uk/) gathering useful civic information and sources in one place.

By day I work in charity administration and project development at [The Kings Centre Southall](https://kingscentresouthall.org.uk/).

I also build and maintain online communities and small web tools. That includes the long-running [Top 100](https://smtop100.blog/) multiplayer football-management world and [Helping Hand](https://hand.davidmarsden.info/), a collection of human-first publishing tools for getting material from paper, tablets and local files onto the web with as little machinery in the way as possible.

And, after a break of roughly thirty years, I am once again making music with [HOVERCRAFT](https://hovercraft.band/), the punk band I helped form in Grimsby in the mid-1990s.

## This site

This site is the personal bit.

The [blog](/blog/) is where I put notes, observations, longer pieces, family life, politics, music, technology, football and whatever else has caught my attention. [Favourite Photos](/photos/) is a deliberately small selection from the much larger photo archive, and the [Library](/library/) records what I’ve been reading.

If you are looking for something older, the [Archive](/archive/) is the complete chronological record.

I’ve been publishing things on the web for a long time. Part of the point of keeping this site is simply that the web is better when people own a little corner of it themselves.
