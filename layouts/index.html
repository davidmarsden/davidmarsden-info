About

I’m based in Southall, west London. I’m an investigative journalist, community researcher, web tinkerer, former, and apparently current-again, punk musician, and administrator of online football worlds that should probably have died decades ago but stubbornly refuse to.

Much of my current writing concerns local government, development, environment and community power in Southall and Ealing.

By day I work in charity administration and project development at King’s Centre Southall.

This is my personal website. The views, writing and projects here are my own unless explicitly stated otherwise.
