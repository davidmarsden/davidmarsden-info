Football worlds

I’ve been running online multiplayer football-management communities for many years. These are the worlds, archives and tools that have grown out of them.

The original world · since 2015

Top 100

Managers. Stories. A bigger game. One hundred clubs, five divisions, cups, records and more than a decade of community history — now brought together as one connected Top 100 family.

Sister world · gateway

Top 100 Regen

Same DNA. New generation. Launched for Top 100’s tenth anniversary, Regen is the sister game world and gateway into the wider community: 100 clubs, five divisions and its own managers, seasons and rule variations.

In development

The Beautiful Game

A new multiplayer online football-management game.

Data

The Pink Final

The companion club and player database for The Beautiful Game.
