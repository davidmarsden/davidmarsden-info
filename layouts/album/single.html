About this record

This is not an attempt to turn a scattered set of recordings into something grander than it is. It is simply an album-shaped home for songs I have made over the years: originals, covers, rewrites, football songs, political songs, book-inspired songs and things that escaped from the internet.

Some began as blog posts, some as jokes, some as grief, some as arguments and some by borrowing somebody else's song and seeing where it went.
